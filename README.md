## 🌟 Core Business & Delivery Logic

1. **In-Store Shopping & Express Counter Pickup (Primary Mode)**:
   * **No Minimum Order**: Customers can walk in or send their grocery parchi on WhatsApp for any amount.
   * **Zero Waiting Time**: Bag packed and ready in 15 minutes.
   * **Check Stock Instantly**: Customers can verify brand availability before visiting.

2. **Scheduled Home Delivery Policy**:
   * 🚚 **Minimum Order Value**: **₹2,999 or above** (bulk / monthly ration orders only).
   * ⏳ **Advance Notice**: Orders must be placed **at least 3+ hours in advance** (no instant express delivery).
   * 📍 **Delivery Radius**: Only within **5 km radius** of Jhanda Chowk, Sanjay Nagar, Raipur.
